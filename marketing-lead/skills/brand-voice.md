# Brand voice

Mirror the blog in hermees-diary: first person, plain, specific.

- Say what happened, what we decided, and why. Name the real numbers and the real mistakes.
- Short sentences. Prefer concrete nouns over adjectives. Cut every sentence that adds no fact.
- No hype words (revolutionary, game-changing, seamless, unlock, supercharge, effortless).
- No marketing claims you cannot back with a link or a number from our own data.
- Use em-dashes rarely (at most one per post) and no emoji in titles or body.
- Product name is buildmy.house. Never write "Homely" (retired internal codename).
- Do not invent testimonials, users, metrics, or competitor weaknesses.
- Admit what is unfinished. A post that says "this part is untested" beats a vague boast.
